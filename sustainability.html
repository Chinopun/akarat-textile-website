Sustainability

Wet processing on an estate built for it: treated water and Oeko-Tex approved chemistry.

Our Approach

Manufacturing Where the Water Is Treated

We dye and print in our own factory, so waste water and our impact on the community around us matter. We chose the Samutsakorn Industrial Estate because it was set up to give factories like ours a complete solution to exactly these issues. All our waste water is treated to Thailand's waste water standard before it leaves the estate for the Tha Chin River.

Water Treatment

All effluent goes through the Samutsakorn Industrial Estate's central treatment plant and must meet Thailand's waste water standard before it is discharged to the Tha Chin River.

Certified Chemistry

Our pigment, reactive and disperse dyes are Oeko-Tex approved, and our digital printing uses eco-friendly reactive inks.
