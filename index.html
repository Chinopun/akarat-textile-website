Who We Are

A Family of Weavers,
Built on Quality

Akarat Textile Industry was founded in 1987 in Prapradaeng, Samutprakarn, weaving wide-width greige fabric for bed sheet makers. In 1995 we moved to the Samutsakorn Industrial Estate and built a fully integrated mill covering weaving, dyeing, printing and finishing.

Everything happens on our own site. Yarn comes in and finished fabric goes out — warping, sizing, weaving, singeing, bleaching, mercerising, dyeing, rotary and digital printing, finishing, inspection and packing are all ours. One point of accountability, and no fabric shuttling between sub-contractors.

What We Make

Our Product Range

Wide-width woven fabric from 44" to 130" — greige, dyed or printed — in plain, twill, satin, dobby and jacquard, up to 500 thread count. We also take commission dyeing and printing on customers' own fabric.

Raw & Greige Fabric

Loomstate wide-width fabric from 108 Sulzer projectile and Toyota Airjet looms, 44" to 130" wide.

Finished Fabric

Cold pad batch and jigger dyeing, Zimmer rotary printing to 16 colours, and direct reactive digital printing.

Woven Fabric

Plain, twill, satin, dobby and jacquard, in counts from 20s to 100s and up to 500 thread count.

Hospital Products

Fabric and bed linen for hospitals across Thailand, in the standard 72"–122" bedding widths.

Hotel & Hospitality

Bed linen for major hotel groups in Thailand and overseas — about 30% of everything we make.

Functional Fabrics

Water repellent, flame retardant, anti-bacterial (Sanitized) and anti-dust-mite (Actigard) finishes.

Why Akarat

Built on Decades of Trust

Vertically integrated, Oeko-Tex Standard 100 certified and Disney FAMA audited, with nearly four decades of wide-width experience behind every order.

🏭

In-House Manufacturing

Warping, sizing, weaving, singeing, bleaching, mercerising, dyeing, rotary and digital printing, steaming, washing, finishing, inspection and packing — every step on our own site in Samutsakorn.

🌐

Global Export Experience

About 70% of our output is sold in Thailand and 30% is exported — to the USA, Japan, Vietnam, Malaysia and the UAE.

✂️

Custom Orders

Fabric is made to your construction, composition, width, thread count, colour and print design. We also dye and print on greige fabric you supply yourself.

🏆

Quality Assured

Greige is inspected on the 4-point system before it enters wet processing, colour is verified on a DataColor spectrophotometer before submission, and every roll is inspected again before packing.
