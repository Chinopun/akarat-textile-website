Category 01

Digital Printing — Direct Reactive

The largest cotton reactive digital printing capacity in Thailand — 500,000 yards a month on Atexco high-speed machines running 48-head and 32-head Kyocera print heads from Japan, up to 320 cm wide. Printing direct with reactive dye fixes the colour into the fibre rather than leaving it on the surface, so a printed sheet washes and wears like a dyed one. There are no screens to engrave: a design can carry unlimited colours with no repeat limit, and a sampling run is as straightforward to put on the machine as a production run. Printed cloth is steamed, washed and finished on our own line.

Category 04

Woven Fabric by Construction

We weave plain, twill, satin, dobby and jacquard constructions in counts from 20s to 100s, up to 500 thread count. Standard 100% cotton constructions run from 20x20/63x61 through to 80x100/230x(125+125), with poly-cotton and 100% polyester microfiber also available.

Weave TypeDescription
Plain WeaveThe simplest over-under construction. Hard-wearing and dimensionally stable — our workhorse for hotel and hospital sheeting.
Twill WeaveA diagonal rib. Drapes more softly than plain weave and hides creasing; used for heavier sheeting and shirting.
Satin WeaveLong floats on the face give a smooth, lustrous surface. Used for higher thread-count sateen bedding.
DobbySmall geometric figures woven on a dobby head — texture and pattern without printing.
JacquardLarge woven-in designs on the jacquard loom, made to a customer's own artwork.
