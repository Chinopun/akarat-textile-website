About Akarat Textile

From a weaving shed in Prapradaeng to a fully integrated mill in the Samutsakorn Industrial Estate.

Our History

Weaving Wide Width Since 1987

Akarat Textile Industry was established in 1987 in Prapradaeng, Samutprakarn. We started as a weaver specialising in wide-width fabric for bed sheets.

At that time very few mills in Thailand could bleach or print wide-width fabric, so customers had to move goods between factories for each stage, and quality and delivery suffered for it. In 1995 we moved to the Samutsakorn Industrial Estate, chosen for its central waste water treatment, and opened our own fully integrated mill.

Today Akarat runs 108 looms and employs around 380 people on a 21-rai site with 20,000 square metres of workshop space. We weave 600,000 yards a month and can dye and finish up to 1.5 million. Annual turnover is about 1,200 million baht.

We hold Thailand’s largest cotton reactive digital printing capacity, 500,000 yards a month. Alongside bed sheet manufacturing for hotels and retail, we supply fabric to the apparel trade: designer labels in Thailand as well as export customers.

Our Facilities

Every Process Under One Roof

Our plant sits on 21 rai in the Samutsakorn Industrial Estate, with 20,000 square metres of workshop space. We chose the estate because it was built to handle the waste water that dyeing and printing produce.

Weaving runs on 82 Sulzer projectile and 26 Toyota Airjet 710/810 looms in a temperature and humidity controlled hall, at 320–700 picks per minute with tuck-in selvedge. Wet processing is Osthoff singeing, Babcock continuous bleaching, mercerising, two Kusters cold pad batch ranges and ten jiggers. Printing is Zimmer rotary and Atexco high-speed digital. Finishing is on Monforts stenters, with Kusters calendering, Lafer sueding and Muzzi sanforizing.
