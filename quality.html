Our Standards

Quality Assurance

Inspected at every stage, from greige on the 4-point system to a final check before packing.

Our Philosophy

Quality is Built In, Not Inspected In

Because weaving, wet processing, printing and finishing all happen in our own factory, a fault found at any stage can be traced straight back to the process that caused it, and fixed there rather than sorted out at despatch.

All greige fabric is inspected on the 4-point system before it goes anywhere near bleaching. Colour is checked on a DataColor spectrophotometer before submission for approval, and our Stork automatic dispenser mixes to a precise weight so repeat orders match the original.

Our Process

Quality Control Process

Quality control runs the length of the line, not just at the end.

1

Greige Fabric Inspection

Every roll of loomstate fabric is inspected on the 4-point system before it is allowed into wet processing.

2

In-Process Control

Checks through singeing, bleaching, mercerising and dyeing for shade, absorbency and evenness. On the print line, screen registration and colour are checked at start-up and through the run.

3

Colour Matching & Approval

Colour is read on a DataColor spectrophotometer and compared against the customer's standard before we submit for approval. The Stork automatic dispenser mixes to a precise weight, so repeat orders match the original.

4

Final Inspection Before Packing

Fabric is inspected again after stentering, calendering and sanforizing, then packed on tubes, as double-fold bolts, or as precuts.
